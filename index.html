📜 Mission briefing

From: Mia

Subject: My birthday party!

Hi!

I'm having a birthday party this Saturday at 3 p.m. at my house. Can you come? What food should we prepare, and what games can we play?

Write back soon!

Mia 🎈

Your mission: reply to Mia. Say whether you can come, suggest food, suggest games, and end your email appropriately.

Model email (example)

Hi Mia,

Thanks for inviting me to your birthday party. I can come on Saturday, and I'm really excited! I think we should prepare pizza, sandwiches and orange juice. We could also have a chocolate cake. For games, we can play musical chairs and a treasure hunt. I'll bring some snacks to share with everyone. It will be fun to celebrate together.

See you on Saturday!

Best wishes,
Alex
